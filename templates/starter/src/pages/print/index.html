The print design system

How the brand meets paper, vinyl, and board: stationery, collateral, desk and office pieces, and event display. It uses the Brand view's mark, type, and palette, and adds what the screen never needs: trim, bleed, safe areas, stock, and ink.

The template scale

Every template in Print, Digital, and Social ships in three steps on the same grid. Choose the step that fits the setting: a letter is Minimal, a seminar handout is Accent, and a booth in a busy hall is Bold. Do not mix steps inside one piece. A set, such as a card and its matching letterhead, may use different steps.

StepSurfaceAccentMark
MinimalQuiet and correspondence-grade. White or natural stock and ink-colored type; the paper does the work.One hairline or rule. No accent fills.The full mark in color.
AccentRecognizable at arm's length. A band of ink or accent frames the content.Eyebrows and one rule. The accent never fills more than about a tenth of the face.The full mark: color on paper, reversed inside the band.
BoldA full-bleed ink flood, or full-bleed photography where the format has room. For rooms, not envelopes.Display type and keylines in the accent.The full mark reversed.

Principles

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Brand owns the identity.Mark files, type families, and colors come from the Brand view. Print never adds a typeface or a color; it only defines how those assets meet ink and stock.
Paper is a surface.Prefer natural or ivory stock to a printed tint. A tint across a whole sheet muddies type and shows banding on digital presses.
Readable at arm's length, then across a room.Body text on letters, brochures, and worksheets is at least 12 pt with generous line spacing, fill-in items are 15 pt, and running text is upright. Only supporting lines go smaller: contact lines, running heads, pad footers, and fine print, and nothing below 7.5 pt. Banner headlines read from about 12 m (40 ft).
Write on paper, never on ink.Anything a reader fills in, ticks, circles, or signs sits on white or ivory: write-in lines, tick boxes, notes, calendar dates, date fields, signature lines. Bands and floods carry only printed type. Ballpoint and pencil vanish on ink.
Compliant by default.Advertising pieces carry the brand's name and address and follow the claim rules of its field, the same as on social. When in doubt, send it for review.
Ink. Record the press build of each color token here once it is proofed: CMYK, the nearest spot color, and where each is used. Keep floods at or below 300% total ink, and never set small type in a rich build.

Formats

Every template page sizes its previews from the print group in src/formats.json. Bleed extends past the trim line; live type and the mark stay inside the safe area.

Previews

A sheet is a print canvas (canvas--print) sized from the catalog, so one preview unit is one inch and type is set in points. It is composed from the same pieces as a screen canvas, in src/styles/canvas.css. Turn on the guides to see the safe area.
