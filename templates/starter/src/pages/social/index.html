The social design system

How the brand shows up on social channels. It inherits the identity from the Brand view and adapts it to fixed-canvas, mobile-first surfaces, then layers on the platform specs, the post and event templates, and the compliance rules every post passes before it goes out.

Principles

This view does not redefine the brand; it translates it. Colors, fonts, the mark, and voice are owned by Brand. This view governs how those assets behave on a square post or a vertical story.

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
One brand, every channel.A post is recognizable before the mark is read: the same palette, the same display type, the same accent. Never introduce a social-only color or typeface.
Platform-native, not platform-generic.Respect each channel's dimensions, safe areas, and reading behavior. A document carousel and a video thumbnail are different crafts; design for where each is seen.
Mobile-first legibility.Most impressions are on a phone. Type, contrast, and the focal point must hold at thumbnail scale before a post is approved at full size.
Compliant by default.Claims, testimonials, and results follow the rules of the brand's field. When in doubt, a post goes to review before it goes out.

Canvases

Every event image, post, and ad is sized from the social group in src/formats.json, and a template is built from the same pieces as Digital DS (src/styles/canvas.css). A story keeps its message out of the bands the platform covers with its own interface; turn on the guides to see them.
