The digital design system

Paid ads, event banners, and promotional graphics for screens: everything placed in front of people who have not visited the website yet, and the banners that bring them to an event. It uses the Brand view's mark, type, and palette, and adds pixel sizes, safe areas, file weights, and the rules each placement brings. Meta and LinkedIn ads live in Social DS.

How this is organized

Each page is one placement family. Every template on it ships in the three steps of the template scale, and one step runs across a campaign set, so the two units a person sees on the same day look related.

AdvertisingThe standard display units and the image and text assets for responsive campaigns.

Owned ChannelsEmail headers and signature banners, the website's promotional hero and strip, and business profile posts.

Delivery rules

RuleWhat it means in practice
File typeJPG for anything with a photo, PNG for flat color and type. Export at exactly the listed pixel size unless the format says 2×. Never upload SVG or WebP to an ad platform unless its specification lists them.
WeightStay under the listed limit. Export a photo at about 80% JPG quality and check the file size before upload, not after a rejection.
Alt textWhere a placement takes alt text, write the message, not a description of the picture. Ad platforms take no alt text, so the message must also be in the ad's text fields.
The mark on every assetEvery paid or promotional asset carries the full brand mark from the Brand view. A monogram is never the only identification.
DisclaimersWhere the brand's field requires a disclaimer line, it appears wherever it fits at a legible size; a format too small to hold it is marked in the catalog and is not used where the line is required.
Safe areasKeep the mark and the copy inside each format's safe area. The guides on any template page show it, with any band the platform covers.
Shared copyA campaign reads word for word the same on every channel: the ad, the banner, the event cover, and the post take their headline, date, and call to action from one place, never retyped per template.
