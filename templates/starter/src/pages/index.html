How this is organized

Each view is one audience's slice of the system. Brand holds the essentials every surface shares. Web DS applies them to interfaces and email, Digital DS to ads and campaign assets, Social DS to social channels, and Print DS to paper. Every asset format those views produce is sized from one catalog, src/formats.json. A planned page is declared in the navigation and not yet authored. Designs are whole pages of the eventual website, authored here on the system's own stylesheets and ported to production by engineers.
